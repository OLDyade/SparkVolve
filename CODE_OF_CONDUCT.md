# Community conduct

We welcome researchers and developers with different backgrounds and experience.

- Discuss mechanisms, evidence and tradeoffs respectfully.
- Credit upstream authors and distinguish observations from hypotheses.
- Keep personal information, credentials, customer data and private evaluation material out of public discussions.
- Harassment, discrimination, threats and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation, considering context and proportionality. Report concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. For security defects, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not expose sensitive details in a public issue.
